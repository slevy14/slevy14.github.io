## Improv

-------------
I have been performing and teaching improvisational theater since 2015 on a variety of teams. Currently, I'm a member of [Fantastiprov](https://www.instagram.com/fantastiprov/), Occidental College's one and only improv team. You can see some of our filmed shows [here](http://www.youtube.com/@Fantastiprov). I also take classes at the Upright Citizen's Brigade theater in LA!
