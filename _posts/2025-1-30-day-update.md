Spent a lot of today looking for jobs and being stressed about looking for jobs. But improv is always a fun way to let off some of that steam and hone a craft that I love! Big things from class today:
- **Framing:** Make it super super clear what the Game of the scene is! This is super necessary to make it clear to your scene partner and the backline, and basically necesary for a second beat.
- **Time Dash & Analagous:** Two kinds of scenes for second beats -- either one of the same characters later, or completely different characters in a similar scenario. Either way playing the same game!

A note to myself is to really work on justification -- why am I in the scene, why is playing the game important, and how can I play big enough to keep it going?
